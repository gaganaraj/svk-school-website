About the School

Education that builds the whole child

Sri Vivekananda Vidya Kendra is managed by the Swami Vivekananda Vidya Kendra trust on Sri Ramakrishna Road, M.V. Extension, Hoskote. Since 1994, we have combined a strong academic base with discipline, culture and service.

  • Karnataka State syllabus, Nursery to Class 10
  • Value-based education inspired by Swami Vivekananda
  • Caring teachers and a safe, supervised campus
  • School transport for pick-up and drop

Our Story

Serving Hoskote since 1994

Sri Vivekananda Vidya Kendra is managed by the Swami Vivekananda Vidya Kendra trust. From Nursery to Class 10, we follow the Karnataka State syllabus while keeping Indian values at the heart of every classroom.

Our campus is on Sri Ramakrishna Road, M.V. Extension, Hoskote.
